# Interaction

<Lead>Every way to open the menu, and everything a press might really be.</Lead>

A finger that moves is scrolling. One that stays put opens the menu. Try both below.

<Example name="scroll-or-press" />

## Hold to open

Hold still for 450ms and the item lifts. It works with a finger or the left mouse button. The press is cancelled if you let go early, move more than 8px, or the page scrolls under a resting finger.

A short press is left alone, so the trigger's own `onClick` still fires on a plain click or tap.

## Slide to pick

Keep holding once the menu is up and slide onto an option. The item under your finger gets `data-highlighted`, and letting go picks it. Let go over nothing and the menu stays open.

## Tap through

Let go straight after the menu opens, and it stays up. Tap an item to pick it, or anywhere outside to dismiss.

## Right-click

A right-click or Ctrl+click opens the menu right away, in place of the browser's own. The iOS callout and the Android context menu are kept out of the way too.

## Keyboard

The trigger is focusable. If it isn't already, it gets a `tabindex`.

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter`, `Space` | On the trigger, opens with focus on the first item. On an item, picks it |
| `ContextMenu`, `Shift+F10` | On the trigger, opens with focus on the first item |
| `ArrowDown`, `ArrowUp` | Moves between items, and wraps around |
| `Home`, `End` | Moves to the first or last item |
| `Escape` | Closes, and returns focus to the trigger |

## Screen readers

Holding still isn't an option with a screen reader, so its activation opens the menu straight away, with focus on the first item. The trigger has `aria-haspopup` and `aria-expanded`, and the menu and its items have `menu` and `menuitem` roles.

## Text selection

With a mouse, dragging across a trigger selects its text as usual, and a press on a selection won't open the menu. On touch, a held finger doesn't start a selection.

## Good to know

- While the menu is up, the page can't scroll and everything else is inert.
- The click that follows a long press is swallowed, so it can't land on whatever is underneath.
- Keys pressed inside a link or field in the trigger stay theirs.
- With reduced motion, the springs are skipped.
