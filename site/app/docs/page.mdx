# Hello World

Welcome to the emerse docs. emerse lets you press and hold an item to lift it above a blurred page, with a menu next to it. Keep your finger down and slide onto an option to pick it in one motion, or let go and tap through the menu afterward.

There's more to come. For now, here's the smallest one you can make:

```tsx
import { Emerse } from "emerse/react";

<Emerse.Root>
	<Emerse.Trigger>…message…</Emerse.Trigger>
	<Emerse.Overlay />
	<Emerse.Menu>
		<Emerse.Item onSelect={reply}>Reply</Emerse.Item>
		<Emerse.Item onSelect={copy}>Copy</Emerse.Item>
	</Emerse.Menu>
</Emerse.Root>;
```
